## Formulas and exact numerical anchors

`TC=F+cq`; `MC=c`; `ATC=F/q+c`; `R=pq`; `π=R−TC`. These are standard model algebra, applied to supplied inputs.

- Q2: `F=€200,000`, `c=€0.10/download`, `q=500,000 downloads` → `TC=€250,000`, `ATC=€0.50/download`, `MC=€0.10/download`.

## Visual intuition and constructed dialogue

The Q15 table has opposing tastes down each consumer's column but equal column totals. Demand for a song is a step function: between WTP thresholds quantity stays fixed, so checking thresholds finds the optimum. The bundle has four buyers through €12 and zero above it.

> Student: “Every customer values both songs at €12, so separate sales earn €48?”
> Coach: “Their **sum** is €12. Separate sales require a uniform price for each song; count buyers at each candidate price.”

> Student: “Training sellers adds value, so knowledge transfer is value-increasing in Q6?”
> Coach: “It can have several effects, but this source classifies reduced learning/setup burden as cost-decreasing. Explain that mechanism for this question.”
